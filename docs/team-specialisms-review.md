# Team specialisms — clinic review list

**Status: awaiting sign-off. Do not cut over until this is signed.**

`worksWith` and `worksOn` in `src/content/team/*.md` are rendered on the team
cards so a parent can scan who handles their situation instead of reading eight
bios. Every value below was **restructured from text the clinic already
published** on this site — nothing was inferred beyond it, and nothing was added
from outside knowledge.

These are statements about a named clinician's scope of practice. They carry the
same weight as a fee. Please confirm, correct, or strike each one.

## Rules applied when deriving

1. **An approach is not a presentation.** "Works in a trauma-informed way" was
   *not* recorded as treating trauma. This is why Laura's `worksOn` has one
   entry despite a long bio — the rest of her bio describes frameworks
   (narrative, schema, ACT, feminist, grief-and-loss), not conditions treated.
2. **The clinic's own words where possible.** "Young people" was kept rather
   than narrowed to "teenagers"; "across the lifespan" became "All ages".
3. **Capped at five.** Where a bio listed more than five presentations, the
   five most likely to be searched for were kept. Every omission is listed
   below so nothing was silently dropped.
4. **Practitioners only.** Support staff (Josie, Natasha) and the dogs (Reea,
   Samson) have no values and render without these rows.
