The Circle of Security parenting program is for parents who want to
understand their child better. It is built on attachment research: decades of
studies on the bond between a child and the people who care for them, and how
a secure bond supports a child's development. The program gives you a simple
map of what your child needs from you, and helps you notice those needs, even
when your child's behaviour hides them.

## What the program is

Rather than teaching techniques or scripts, the program looks at the
relationship between you and your child, and treats that relationship as the
strongest tool you have. It takes into account that every family and every
child is different.

A central idea is "good enough" parenting. Perfect parenting does not exist,
and trying to achieve it leaves parents discouraged and burnt out. The program
asks you to reflect on who you are as a parent, what you bring to time with
your child, and how to give your child the security that their development
rests on.

Secure attachment is linked with children's later wellbeing, self-esteem and
adjustment. The program is run around the world, and its principles apply to
neurodivergent parents and children as well.

## Who it suits

The program is for parents and carers of children aged 4 months to 12 years.
It may suit you if you are unsure whether you are a "good enough" parent, if
you feel you do not understand your child, or if your child's behaviour has
started to feel like the problem. You do not need a referral, and your child
does not attend.

## Next dates

Groups run in intakes rather than continuously. Call reception to find out
when the next group starts, and to register your interest.

## Who runs it

The program is run by a Therapy House clinician. Reception can tell you who is
running the next group.

## In a group, or on your own

The group runs in person at the clinic in The Gap. There are advantages to
being in a group, but the program can also be delivered to one or two parents
outside the group setting, as a separate request that depends on the
clinician's availability. Ask reception whether that, or a video option, is
possible for you.
