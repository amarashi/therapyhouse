Therapy House is a psychology clinic in The Gap, Brisbane, and you do not
need to live in Brisbane to see one of our psychologists. A telehealth
session is a session with a psychologist by video: an online psychologist
appointment, held over a video call, from wherever you live in Australia.

## How video sessions work

A video session runs for the same 50 minutes as a session in the room. When
you book, reception sends you the details for joining the call. You need:

- a phone, tablet or computer with a camera and a microphone;
- an internet connection that can carry a video call;
- a quiet room where you will not be overheard or interrupted. A parked car
  or a shared office is not private enough.

Headphones help if other people are at home. Your psychologist has the same
duty of confidentiality on a video call as in the room.

## Who video sessions suit

Video suits people who live too far from The Gap to travel each week, and
people for whom the trip itself is the hard part: a long illness, a disability,
young children at home, shift work, or no car. It also suits people who
started sessions in the room and then moved away, and people who simply find
it easier to talk from home.

If you are unwell on the day of a session, the clinic asks you to keep the
appointment by video rather than cancel.

Video is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 000.
For support at any hour, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

## Children and teenagers by video

With children, much of the work is with their parents, and parent sessions
are a core part of therapy. A parent session can be held by video. If your
child is sick on the day, the clinic asks you to keep the appointment as a
parent-only online session.

Whether video suits your child depends on their age and on what is going on,
and the psychologist will talk that through with you. Reception can tell you
which clinicians have video times for children and teenagers.

## Anywhere in Australia

Psychologists in Australia are registered nationally with the Psychology
Board of Australia, through Ahpra (the Australian Health Practitioner
Regulation Agency). Once registered, a practitioner can practise anywhere in
Australia, so a video session with Therapy House works the same way in
Cairns, Melbourne or Perth.

Sessions are booked in Brisbane time. Queensland does not use daylight saving,
so from October to April a session at 10am in Brisbane is 11am in Sydney,
Melbourne, Canberra and Hobart.
