Central Queensland has far fewer psychologists per person than the rest of the
state: 72 for every 100,000 people in 2023, where Queensland as a whole had
115. If you live in Rockhampton and want a psychologist, a video session with
Therapy House in Brisbane is one way around that gap.

## Fewer psychologists in Central Queensland

The primary health network for the region, Country to Coast QLD, set those
figures out in its 2025 to 2028 assessment. It also noted that people in
Central Queensland turn up at hospital and emergency departments for mental
health reasons at high rates, which it reads as a sign that care in the
community is missing. The regional health service's own assessment makes the
link plainly: more clinicians would mean shorter waits.

Rockhampton does have a free Medicare Mental Health Centre on William Street,
open since January 2025. For ongoing, weekly therapy with the same
psychologist, video sessions are another option.

## Video sessions from Rockhampton and the Capricorn Coast

Whether you are in the city, out at Gracemere or up at Yeppoon, the setup is
the same: a device with a camera and a microphone, a reliable internet
connection, and somewhere private to talk for 50 minutes. Rockhampton runs on
Brisbane time, with no daylight saving on either end.

## Children and teenagers

headspace Rockhampton on Alma Street is open to 12 to 25 year olds. For a
younger child, sessions with a parent are a core part of therapy, and a parent
session can be held by video from home in Rockhampton. Reception can tell you
which of our clinicians offer video sessions for children and teenagers.

## Marriage and relationship counselling by video

Partners in Rockhampton looking for marriage counselling can have sessions
together by video, side by side at one screen. That also helps when work
takes one partner away for weeks at a time: sessions can be booked for the
weeks you are both home. Medicare does not cover couple sessions.

## A GP referral in Rockhampton

A Medicare rebate starts with your GP. Book a longer appointment with your
regular Rockhampton GP, or with the practice you are enrolled in through
MyMedicare, and ask for a Mental Health Treatment Plan and a referral to a
psychologist at Therapy House. A paediatrician or psychiatrist in
Rockhampton can also refer you directly. Send the referral to us before your
first session by video.
