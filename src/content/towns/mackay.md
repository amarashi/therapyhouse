Mackay sits in the Northern Queensland region, where local health
practitioners rated psychology as the biggest workforce gap in a 2023 survey
by Health Workforce Queensland. Young people aged 12 to 25 can see headspace
Mackay for free. For adults, couples and parents, a video session from
home is another way to see a psychologist without waiting for a local
appointment to open up.
