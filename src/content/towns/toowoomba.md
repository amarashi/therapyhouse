Toowoomba is 127 kilometres west of Brisbane, about an hour and a half by car
each way. That is close enough for a day trip, and too far to drive down the
range every week for a 50-minute session. A psychologist by video gives
Toowoomba residents a Brisbane clinic without the drive.

## Seeing a psychologist in Toowoomba

The regional needs assessment for the Darling Downs and West Moreton found
that psychology had the largest health workforce gap of any profession in
2022. Health workers told the same assessment that because local services are
thin, people already lean on telehealth.

Toowoomba does have free places to start. Wattle House on Margaret Street
takes walk-ins with no referral, and headspace on Snell Street is there for
12 to 25 year olds. What can be harder to find is a regular, weekly
appointment with one psychologist, or a clinician who works with a
particular age group or problem.

## Video sessions from Toowoomba

You join the call from a laptop, tablet or phone, in a room where nobody can
overhear. The session runs for the same 50 minutes as one in our rooms at The
Gap. Brisbane and Toowoomba share a time zone, so
the time on your booking is the time on your clock.

If you come down to Brisbane now and then, for work or family, you can mix
video sessions with the occasional one in person.

## Children and teenagers

Parents in Toowoomba can have parent sessions by video, which are a large
part of therapy with a young child. Whether an older child or a teenager has
their own sessions by video is something the psychologist will talk through
with you. Reception can tell you which clinicians have video times for your
child's age.

## Couples and relationship counselling by video

Couples sessions can be held by video, with both partners together in front of
one screen. Medicare does not cover couple sessions, so the full fee applies. If one partner is in
Toowoomba and the other works away, video can keep sessions going through the
roster.

## A GP referral in Toowoomba

To claim the Medicare rebate, book a longer appointment with your usual GP in
Toowoomba, or with the practice where you are registered through MyMedicare.
Ask for a Mental Health Treatment Plan and a referral to a psychologist at
Therapy House. A Toowoomba paediatrician or psychiatrist can also refer
directly. Send us the referral before the first video session.
