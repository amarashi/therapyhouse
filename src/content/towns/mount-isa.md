Mount Isa is 1,829 kilometres from Brisbane and 883 kilometres from
Townsville, the closest major city, according to Mount Isa City Council. Since
October 2025 the town has had a free Medicare Mental Health Centre on
Camooweal Street, which needs no appointment or referral. For regular
sessions with a psychologist, a video call removes a very long trip.
