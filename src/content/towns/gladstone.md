Gladstone is in the Central Queensland health service area, where local
practitioners rated psychology as the biggest workforce gap of any
profession in a 2023 Health Workforce Queensland survey. The Medicare Mental
Health Centre on Derby Street takes walk-ins at no cost. For ongoing therapy
with the same psychologist each week, video sessions are an option from
Gladstone.
