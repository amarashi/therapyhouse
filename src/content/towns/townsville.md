Townsville is too far from Brisbane for a weekly trip to a clinic. You can
still see a psychologist at Therapy House:
by video, on a weekday that suits you, from a quiet room at home or at work.

## Why finding a psychologist in Townsville can take time

Adults in the Townsville health service area report more mental health
trouble than Queenslanders overall: almost one in ten lives with a long-term
condition such as depression or anxiety, and the average adult has more
mentally unhealthy days each month than the state average. At the same time,
Health Workforce Queensland rates psychology as the profession the north is
shortest of.

When the region's primary health network asked locals what gets in the way,
they named long waits, too few clinicians, cost and distance. The Medicare
Mental Health Centre on Walker Street, which takes people without a referral,
has been busy since it opened: more than 12,000 occasions of care.

## How a video session runs for someone in Townsville

There is no travel, so a session fits around a shift, a school pick-up or a
posting. You need a device with a camera, a steady connection and a door you
can close. Townsville and Brisbane keep the same clock all year, so there is
no time difference to work out.

A first session is mostly about listening: what is happening, what you have
tried, and what you would like to be different.

## Children and teenagers in Townsville

Young people aged 12 to 25 can also use headspace in Thuringowa Central. For
younger children, therapy often means sessions with the parents as well as the
child, and parent sessions run by video. Reception can say which of our
clinicians see children and teenagers by video.

## Marriage and relationship counselling by video

Couples in Townsville often look for marriage or relationship counselling and
find little nearby. A video session with both of you on the couch, one laptop
between you, is one way to start. If one of you works away or on rotation,
sessions can be planned around the time at home. Medicare does not cover
couple sessions.

## A GP referral in Townsville

Your Townsville GP, or the clinic you are enrolled with through MyMedicare, can
write a Mental Health Treatment Plan and refer you to a Therapy House
psychologist. That is what makes the Medicare rebate payable. A paediatrician
or psychiatrist in Townsville can refer directly too. Email the referral to
the clinic before your first session.
