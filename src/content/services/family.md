Family therapy is for families in distress: when there is a lot of conflict at
home, when one person's difficulties are affecting everyone, or after a change
that the family is struggling to adjust to, such as a separation, a new
step-family, an illness or a loss. Instead of treating one person as "the
problem", it looks at what is happening between people.

## What family therapy is

Families get into patterns. A child acts out, a parent reacts, another parent
steps in, and the same argument plays out again and again, even though nobody
wants it. Family therapy helps everyone see the pattern, understand what each
person is feeling and needing underneath it, and practise responding
differently.

The psychologist makes sure each person gets a turn to speak and to be heard,
including children, who often notice more than adults expect. Sessions are not
about blame. The aim is for the family to communicate more calmly, solve
problems together, and feel closer.

Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), one of the therapies the clinic's
psychologists are trained in, is used with families as well as couples. You
can read about it on [the therapies we use](/therapy/approaches/#eft).

Family therapy is often used alongside individual work. A child may see a
psychologist on their own and the family may also come together for some
sessions. Family therapy is one of the kinds of help described on
[psychology for children and teenagers](/children/).

## Who attends

That depends on the family and on what is going on. Often it is the parents
and the children who live at home, but it might be one parent and one child,
siblings, grandparents, or a step-parent. Not everyone has to come to every
session. The psychologist will talk with you at the start about who should be
in the room, and some sessions may be with the parents alone.

If the strain is mainly between the two of you as partners,
[couples counselling](/therapy/couples/) may suit better. Reception can help
you choose.

## Fees and Medicare

Couples and family sessions are $280 to $350. Medicare does not cover family
sessions, even with a GP referral, so there is no rebate. All fees are on the
[fees page](/fees/).

## How to book

Call reception, describe briefly what is happening in your family, and they
will suggest a clinician and a time that works for the people who need to
attend.
