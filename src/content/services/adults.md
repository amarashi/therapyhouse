A psychologist for adults is someone you can talk to about what is getting in
the way of ordinary life: worry that will not switch off, a mood that has
stayed low, something that happened and still feels present, a loss, or a
relationship under strain. You do not need a diagnosis, or the right words for
it, before you book.

## What adults come to us for

**[Anxiety](/therapy/anxiety/).** Worry, panic, and fears that have started to decide what you do
and do not do. Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD, where unwanted thoughts and
the urge to repeat actions take up a lot of time) is covered on the same page.

**[Depression and low mood](/therapy/depression/).** Feeling flat, tired or hopeless for weeks at a
time, losing interest in things you used to enjoy, or finding it hard to get
through the day.

**[Trauma](/therapy/trauma/).** The after-effects of a frightening or overwhelming event, or of
things that went on for a long time. EMDR (eye movement desensitisation and
reprocessing, a structured therapy for traumatic memories) is one of the
therapies used here.

**[Grief](/therapy/grief/).** After a death, a separation, or another loss that has changed your
life.

**[Couples and relationships](/therapy/couples/).** Couples counselling, for partners who keep
having the same argument or who feel they have lost touch with each other.

Each of these has its own page, and all of them are listed further down.

## ADHD support for adults

ADHD (attention deficit hyperactivity disorder) does not end in childhood, and
many adults are only now recognising it in themselves. Therapy can help with
the day-to-day effects: planning and organisation, starting and finishing
tasks, managing strong emotions, and the self-criticism that often builds up
after years of struggling without knowing why.

Psychologists do not prescribe medication. If you are thinking about
medication, a psychiatrist (a medical doctor who specialises in mental health)
or your GP can talk that through with you, and an assessment report from a
psychologist can be part of that conversation.

## Assessment for adults

If you are wondering whether you have ADHD or are autistic, an assessment
gives you a clear answer and a written report. Read about
[adult ADHD assessment](/assessments/adult-adhd/) and
[adult autism assessment](/assessments/adult-autism/), or see
[how assessment works](/assessments/) for the general process.

## How it works

Individual appointments are 50 minutes. You can come to the clinic at
1290 Waterworks Rd, The Gap, or have sessions
[by video from home](/telehealth/). Call
reception if you are not sure which clinician would suit you; they will talk
it through and suggest someone. Session fees and Medicare rebates are on the [fees page](/fees/).
