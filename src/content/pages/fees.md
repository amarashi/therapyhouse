This page sets out Therapy House's fees and Medicare rebates for psychology
sessions, in person in The Gap, Brisbane, and by video. It covers what a
session costs, how much Medicare pays back when your GP refers you with a
Mental Health Treatment Plan, and how private health insurance and NDIS
funding work.

Therapy House does not bulk bill. Bulk billing means a clinic accepts the
Medicare rebate as the whole payment. Here you pay the session fee, and
Medicare pays part of it back to you.
