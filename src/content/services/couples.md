Couples counselling is for partners who keep having the same argument, who
feel more like housemates than a couple, or who have been hurt and do not know
how to get past it. Every couple has conflict. What makes the difference is
how the two of you deal with it, and that can be learned.

## What couples therapy is here

You come together, and the sessions are about the relationship rather than
about who is right. The psychologist helps each of you say what is really
going on for you, and helps the other hear it, which is often hard to do at
home when emotions run high. You look at the pattern that keeps repeating
between you, for example one person pushing for closeness while the other
pulls away, and you practise a different way of responding.

Couples come at every stage: newly together and deciding whether this is the
right relationship, married for many years, after an affair, after children
have left home, or when one partner is struggling with their own mental
health and it is affecting you both. You do not need to be married.

## Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)

Emotionally Focused Therapy, usually called EFT, is one of the therapies the
clinic's psychologists are trained in, and it was developed for couples. It
starts from the idea that most couple conflict is about
connection: underneath the criticism or the silence, each partner is asking
"are you there for me?". EFT helps you recognise the cycle you get caught in,
understand the feelings and needs underneath it, and respond to each other in
ways that build trust instead of distance. It usually takes a number of
sessions, and the early ones focus on calming the cycle down.

You can read more about EFT and the other approaches used at Therapy House on
[the therapies we use](/therapy/approaches/).

## Parents as a couple

Having children changes a relationship. Sleep, money, housework and very
different ideas about parenting can leave little room for the two of you, and
small resentments can build into a distance that neither of you chose.
Couples therapy can help you work as a team again and make room for your
relationship as well as your family. If the strain is across the whole family,
[family therapy](/therapy/family/) may suit better, and reception can help
you choose.

## Sessions, fees and Medicare

Couples sessions can be held in the room in The Gap or by video, which suits
couples who live outside Brisbane or work different hours. Ask reception how
long couples sessions run and which length suits you.

Couples and family sessions are $280 to $350. Medicare does not cover couple sessions, even with
a GP referral, so there is no rebate. All fees are on the [fees page](/fees/).

To book, call reception or book online. If one of you is unsure about coming,
that is common; reception can talk it through with either of you first.
