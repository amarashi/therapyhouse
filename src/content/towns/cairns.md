A psychologist in Cairns can be hard to get in to see. In mid-2025 one local
psychologist told the Herald Sun that people across the city were waiting two
to three months for a first appointment. Video sessions with Therapy House let
you see a psychologist from Cairns while you wait, or instead of waiting.

## The wait for a psychologist in Cairns

About 170 full-time-equivalent psychologists work in the Cairns local
government area, according to the Australian Psychological Society. The same
report quotes a Cairns practice taking 10 to 15 new referrals every week with
no room to see them quickly, and warns that problems left for months can
become harder to shift.

The Cairns and Hinterland health service's needs assessment puts access to
mental health care among its priorities, and people in its consultation said
mental health care was still lagging behind. In April 2026 a free Medicare
Mental Health Centre opened on Mulgrave Road in Bungalow for people who need
support without a booking.

## Sessions by video, from Cairns

From Edge Hill to Smithfield to Gordonvale, all you need is a quiet room, a
phone, tablet or computer with a camera, and a connection that can hold a
video call. Cairns is on the same time as Brisbane, so a 4pm session is 4pm
for both of you. Sessions run for 50 minutes.

## Children and teenagers

headspace on Grafton Street sees 12 to 25 year olds. If your child is younger,
or you would like a psychologist who works with the whole family, therapy at
Therapy House often includes parent sessions, and those can happen by video
from Cairns. Reception can tell you which clinicians have video times for
children and teenagers.

## Couples counselling by video

For couples in Cairns, a video session means neither of you has to take an
afternoon off to drive to an appointment. You sit together and share one
screen. Medicare does not cover couple
sessions, so the session fee is paid in full.

## A GP referral in Cairns

The Medicare rebate needs a referral. See your usual Cairns GP, or a GP at
the practice you are registered with through MyMedicare, and ask for a Mental
Health Treatment Plan with a referral to a Therapy House psychologist. A
Cairns paediatrician or psychiatrist can refer you directly instead. Send
the referral through before your first video session.
