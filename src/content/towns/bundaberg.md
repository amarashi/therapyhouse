The region's mental health plan for 2025 to 2030 notes that many people in
Bundaberg end up relying on hospital care because early help is hard to get.
Adults can walk into the free Medicare Mental Health Centre on Bourbong
Street without a referral. If you would rather have regular sessions with one
psychologist, including sessions for your child or as a couple, you can have
them by video from Bundaberg.
